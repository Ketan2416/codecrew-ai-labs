How it works

From first call to
live in production

  1. 01

    Discovery week

    A paid week that produces a written specification, an architecture and a real estimate. Build it with us or take the document elsewhere — it is yours either way.

  2. 02

    Design & scope

    Interfaces, data models and interfaces between systems, agreed before code. Each phase gets a fixed price so nothing is a surprise.

  3. 03

    Build in the open

    Weekly demos on a real staging URL. You talk to the engineers writing the code, in a shared channel, for the whole project.

  4. 04

    Launch & look after

    Deployment, monitoring and documented handover — then an ongoing retainer if you want us watching it. Software nobody owns stops working.

Questions

Answers,
before you ask

Something not covered here? Email us and you will get a real reply from a real engineer.

How much does a project cost?

Add your real ranges here — for example: discovery weeks are fixed at X, most builds land between Y and Z, retainers start at W per month. Being specific about money filters out the wrong enquiries and speeds up the right ones.

How long until something is live?

A focused agent or marketing site is typically live within a few weeks of the specification being signed off. Larger platforms ship in phases, with something usable at the end of each one rather than a single launch date at the end.

Who actually does the work?

We do. There is no account manager layer and no handing your project to a subcontractor you never met. The people on your calls are the people writing your code.

Do we own the code?

Yes, entirely, including the repository and infrastructure. Handover includes documentation and every credential. You are never locked in.

What happens after launch?

Either you take it in-house with documentation and a walkthrough, or we stay on a monthly retainer covering monitoring, fixes and small improvements. Both are fine, and we will tell you honestly which one suits your situation.
